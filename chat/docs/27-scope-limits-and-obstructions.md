# Scope limits and obstructions

This file summarizes the limits of the archived investigation. The last search outputs are UNKNOWN; the retained evidence supplies neither a counterexample nor an exhaustive exclusion of the synthesis domains.

## Scope of the implemented searches

The implemented joint synthesis system chooses cell types, port assignments and connections together. It avoids freezing a protected ring whose remaining boundary later proves unrepairable. Learned conflicts retain the complete support of simultaneous vertex-disjoint routes and can generalize across alternative cells. Safe passage patterns, including the exact heptagon profile and theta(2,3,4) exact-one rule, are usable necessary conditions on appropriate simple quotient cycles.

The 86 explicit local escapes do not define an exclusive library: uniform-transmission theorems do not exclude those cells from mixed constructions. The simple-quotient arguments do not automatically cover arbitrary quotient loops or parallel links.

The identification solver explores noncubic graphs by merging pairs of terminals into degree-four vertices. It enforces distinct-neighbor degree constraints after simplification and uses collision-guarded learning. Its collision analysis covers size-two classes, not larger equivalence classes or nonmatching fusion.

## Unresolved domains

The joint synthesis runs did not reach or rule out their one- or three-exposed-terminal targets. Joining the unique deficient vertices of two copies of a power-free one-deficiency graph by a bridge gives a counterexample; the retained runs produced no such graph. The three-terminal domain was not restricted to the closed triangle grammar.

The finite strip, cap and triangle-motif exclusions do not cover all larger or differently connected interfaces. Four-link gluing produced an 18-vertex cell outside the small initial domain, although that cell did not repair the tested phase seeds. Composed spectra and boundary repair constraints were separate obstacles; an individual path gap did not settle either one.

The first alpha-block matching-hitting searches did not cover coordinated surgeries with adjacent removed edges.

The retained conflict-learning certificates validate rejection rules, not exhaustive search coverage. Capped absence calls and timeouts do not establish nonexistence.

## Relation to the full conjecture

Local cycle-count minima and long-gap cubic graphs are partial extremal results. The graph's order determines how many powers must be excluded, and avoiding more initial powers can leave a larger forbidden power present. The retained 370- and 26,550-vertex graphs demonstrate this distinction explicitly.

Neither the failures of these restricted strategies nor the local interface escapes settle Erdős–Gyárfás. No complete finite graph of minimum degree at least three with an all-power simple-cycle absence certificate was obtained.
