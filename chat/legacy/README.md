# Retained early-stage material

These files predate the five complete research bundles. Their internal bytes and relative historical subpaths are preserved. Central portable graph copies and fresh checks are in `../data/graphs/`.

`b0_runs/continue12/s403.g6` is the61-vertex seed. `lift3_runs/` and `lift3_cap_runs/` contain the original lifted graphs. `work2/` retains the uncapped lift, one order-eight-cap representative, the cap summary and a303,008-cycle checkpoint. `replay/edge_counts_219531.tsv` contains all edges and incidence counts of the219,531 graph, not just its busiest edges.

The sources document particular historical algorithms. They do not by themselves reconstruct every subsequently reported search scope. Absolute scratch paths appearing in a log or summary are historical identifiers; only files actually listed in this tree exist here. Do not interpret absent candidate files as graphs with zero cycles.

See `../docs/02-alpha-block-and-voltage-lifts.md`, notes03–04, and `../CORRECTIONS.md` for the evidence limits.
